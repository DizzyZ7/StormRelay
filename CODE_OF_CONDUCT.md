# Code of Conduct

StormRelay follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles: be respectful, inclusive, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in MAINTAINERS.md. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants based on severity and pattern. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible.

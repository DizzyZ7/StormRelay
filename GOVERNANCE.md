# Governance

StormRelay is currently maintained under a benevolent-maintainer model with transparent public decisions. Routine changes use pull-request consensus. Architectural, compatibility, security-boundary, and governance changes require an RFC and ADR.

Maintainers may merge after required checks and review. Emergency security fixes may be prepared privately and merged with abbreviated public discussion, followed by a disclosure and retrospective when safe.

Maintainer nominations consider sustained contribution quality, review judgment, reliability/security understanding, community conduct, and availability. Maintainers may step down voluntarily or be removed for prolonged inactivity, abuse of access, or repeated policy violations through unanimous agreement of the remaining active maintainers.
